## Patterns
- P-01: WHEN configuring browser tests DO keep configuration evaluation side-effect free and prepare shared fixtures in one dedicated lifecycle hook.
- P-02: WHEN collecting browser-test failure evidence DO capture in framework teardown before fixture disposal; in-test finally blocks cannot survive a whole-test timeout.
- P-03: WHEN publishing browser-test evidence attachments DO validate format and record identity before publication, and fail closed for missing or mismatched artifacts.
