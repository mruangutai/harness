chore(FEAT-03): status is in_review, not awaiting_user

`awaiting_user` is the question round-trip token: it tells the main
session to ask the user, write an answers file, and re-delegate the
orchestrator. That is not what this feature needs. The briefing is
written, and what comes back is a ship / fix / re-scope / stop decision —
which is `in_review`. The open questions ride alongside the briefing
rather than routing the return.
