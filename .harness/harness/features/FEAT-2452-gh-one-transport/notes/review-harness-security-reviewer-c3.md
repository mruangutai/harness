## Residual advisories (not regressions)

- **Substance · low · T-01 / B3.** Labels have alphanumeric *edges*, not a DNS length bound. A faulty or substituted gh can still return an authority with a 64-character label or a hostname longer than 253 characters and have its number recorded. Both pins accepted both limits; the authority is never fetched (`factory_gh.py:247–258`; `gh-sync.py:1197–1204,1302–1307`).
- **Substance · low · T-01.** A well-formed foreign owner/repo URL still returns its number even when the create argv names a different repo, measured at both pins. A faulty or substituted gh can therefore supply a number that the caller records against the bound repo; the parser checks shape, not destination identity. Ordinary issue editors do not control `gh issue create` stdout (`factory_gh.py:254–269`; `gh-sync.py:1295–1307`).
