### Finding — substance · medium · T-01 (logger; new reach via T-04)

With `HARNESS_GH_COST_LOG=1`, an operator who resolves a private tracker ticket with `--body` now leaves the **entire** decision comment in `.harness/logs/gh-cost-*.jsonl`. A local reader or collector with access to that file, but not the private ticket, can read the comment, including any sensitive material it contains. The base wayfind subprocess did not measure these calls. At the pin, `wayfind.py:299–301,318–320` passes the body through `factory_gh.py:214–216`, and `gh_cost_log.py:97–105,119–143` serializes argv, truncating only long `-f`/`-F` values. T-02 also newly measures ordinary milestone creation, recording up to 80 characters of each `-f` field. The log is opt-in and gitignored, which limits reach but does not remove the local copy. Redact body values at the cost-log boundary; keep command and cost metadata. No automatic logging of process streams, auth output, inherited tokens, or authorization headers was found in the scoped call sites.

The pinned T-04 receipt records byte-exact forwarding. `GhError` keeps streams in memory; ordinary display uses an error summary, including at most the first captured line from the selected stream in the shared exit diagnostic, rather than dumping both streams.
