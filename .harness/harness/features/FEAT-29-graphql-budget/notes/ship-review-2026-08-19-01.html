What the two send-backs bought

Both were spent on one assertion in T-04: that an unrelated gh failure does not produce the budget message. That assertion is the only thing pinning the message to rate-limit failures rather than to every failure. It was green before the change and green after, and had never executed under a mutation until cycle three. That is exactly the failure mode this project has been finding all week — green, and incapable of going red.

The root cause is worth more than the incident: test-factory-gh.py aborts rather than reddens under any mutant that adds one subprocess call. That single property cost both send-backs and is what makes T-03 unlandable. One cause, two symptoms.
