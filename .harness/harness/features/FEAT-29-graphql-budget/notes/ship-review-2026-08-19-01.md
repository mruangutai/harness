## Why T-03 stopped, in one paragraph

T-03 creates `test-gh-cost-log.py`. `run-unit-tests.sh:40-55` walks every `test-*.py` in the bin
directory and exits 2 for any file listed in neither of its two script arrays — **over the union,
regardless of which `--kind` you ask for.** `run-unit-tests.sh` is not in T-03's `files:`. So landing
T-03 as written would not merely leave T-03 unverifiable; it would make *every* task's `verify:` exit
2 until the file was registered. The lead declined to dispatch rather than spend a member spawn on a
gate that cannot pass, and I verified that premise against the source rather than relaying it. It was
correct, and worse than reported.

## What the two send-backs bought

Both were spent on one assertion in T-04: that an *unrelated* `gh` failure does **not** produce the
budget message. That assertion is the only thing pinning the message to rate-limit failures rather
than to every failure. It was green before the change and green after, and had never executed under a

under any mutant that adds one subprocess call. That single property cost both send-backs *and* is
what makes T-03 unlandable. One cause, two symptoms.
