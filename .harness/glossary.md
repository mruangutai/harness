# Glossary

## Risk grade

The integer from 1 to 5 that a function carries, determined by whichever metric produces the worst grade. Grade 1 is reported as a high-severity finding and grade 2 as med on the existing severity ladder.

## Gated set

The functions a change is responsible for: functions that are new or whose risk grade got worse.

## Driver metric

The one of the three metrics that produced a function's risk grade.

## ABC magnitude

The combined assignment, branch, and condition size of a function.

## Cognitive complexity

A Sonar-style approximation of how hard a function is to follow, not SonarSource's algorithm.

## Cyclomatic complexity

The number of independent paths through a function.
